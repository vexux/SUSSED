# General Fact-or-Cap Content Expansion Plan

This is a strategic planning note, not a second inventory. The authoritative
current question/pack counts and provisional topic coverage are generated by
`npm run report:general-content-coverage`; published question IDs and duplicate
signals are in `npm run report:question-inventory`. The
[`general-topic-ledger.json`](general-topic-ledger.json) is the reconciled
planning index. Do not copy counts or question lists into this document.

## Prioritization principles

- Prefer well-supported, memorable claims in areas with low ledger coverage;
  do not fill a quota with weak or obscure material.
- Balance life science and space-heavy representation with culture/society,
  technology/engineering, and physical science/math when strong evidence and
  playable wording are available.
- Seek variety in subject, source, claim type, completion, and prompt structure.
  Review claim summaries and human repetition notes; normalized duplicate
  signals do not detect paraphrases.
- Keep each prompt self-contained, unambiguous, and suitable for a mixed party
  group. Assess factual support and reuse rights independently of classification.

## Strategic priorities and next batch

- For the next General batch, first scan the latest coverage report and target
  one or more currently low-representation areas only where candidates meet
  evidence and quality standards.
- Include varied claim forms and sources; avoid extending known high-density
  subject clusters without editorial reason.
- Keep the batch in `incoming/` through preview and per-question review. Import
  only after structural, duplicate, evidence, editorial, and rights review.
- After import, classify each published ID and update the ledger through the
  preview/apply maintenance CLI documented in
  [`general-question-generation-guide.md`](general-question-generation-guide.md).

## Unresolved editorial concerns

- Review the existing suspected semantic-overlap entries in the ledger and
  retain their uncertainty until a human comparison resolves them.
- Continue to treat source attribution as unverified metadata until the
  relevant passages are inspected. Rights/reuse remains unknown absent a
  documented assessment.

Revise this plan when strategic priorities or batch direction changes, not
after each question or count change. The report and ledger remain authoritative
for current coverage and identities.
