# Product

SUSSED! lets a group play short rounds of bluffing and voting in a shared browser room. A guest can enter a display name and create or join a room without registering. A host can share a code, invitation link or QR code for private play. Public discovery lets a player choose a joinable lobby by host, game and available seats.

The host selects the game and round count, manages visibility and locking, and starts the match when the lobby meets its readiness and player requirements. Other players ready up and participate from their own screens. Room chat and reactions let members communicate. Hosts can remove or ban a player from their room. Players can report a room member for moderator review.

| Game | Experience | Players to start |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fact or Cap | Invent plausible completions to factual prompts, then vote among the options. | 2–8 |
| Fact or Cap Anime | Play the same rules with an anime question bank. | 2–8 |
| Bruh wtf? | Answer a prompt and vote on other players' anonymous answers. | 3–8 |

When account services are configured, players can manage profiles and handles, search for friends, exchange friend requests and send in-app game invitations. Guest seats and account profiles have separate identities. A same-tab refresh can recover a room while the server still holds the seat. Restarting the backend ends live sessions.

This overview covers implemented experiences. The [game library](../server/gameLibrary.js) models access grants and availability. Purchases and a durable purchase history are outside the experience documented here.
